Coaster math

The cork sheet sits in the craft drawer, the gift list keeps growing, and the craft-fair price was a guess. Coaster math lays coasters out on the sheet and counts the waste, turns your set list into sheets to buy and material cost per set, and adds your hours and margin so the price tag actually pays for the evening. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Yield plan - sheet size and coaster diameter become coasters per sheet and the waste share.
  • Set plan - sets and coasters per set become sheets to buy, material cost and cost per set.
  • Price plan - material, hours at your rate and a margin become break-even and selling price.

Labeled norms

Labeled layout: coasters cut in a simple grid, a circle taking about 78.5% of its square - illustrative, since nesting odd diameters or hand cutting changes the waste. Prices, hours and rates are your own inputs; the shop's price tags and your own clock win.
